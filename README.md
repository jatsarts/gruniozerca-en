# Gruniożerca - English Translation

English translation of the first game in the otherwise puzzle-centric Gruniożerca trilogy. It's an extremely simple arcade game where you collect carrots falling from the sky by changing your color to match said carrots. (Weird, I know.) This was made in a couple of hours with minimal programming experience and some manually proofread DeepL translations (this is not slop I just don't know Polish)

It was originally made for Gramytatywnie, a 24-hour charity auction livestream centered around games with all sorts of special events n' stuff. (At least, that's what Google says...) As of writing, it was last hosted in 2019 (yeesh). It's organized by the Polish gaming magazine [ARHN](https://ar.hn/), and as such the original NES version is Polish. This game is not particularly text-heavy, so you shouldn't be missing much by playing the original, but it's always nice to know what those credits that appear intermittently say.

**NOTE:** `emunes.pl`, a website referenced in-game, now redirects you to *a Polish gambling website.* I have decided to keep the links in for sake of preservation. *I do not condone gambling.*

To play, simply download the latest [release](https://github.com/jatsarts/gruniozerca-en/releases), or build it yourself.
